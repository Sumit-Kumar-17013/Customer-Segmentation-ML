What it does

One profile in, one segment out

Every field below feeds directly into the trained clustering pipeline — nothing here is simulated.

Behavior Analysis

Recency, tenure and web visits are read together to gauge how engaged a customer really is right now.

Purchase Intelligence

Total spend and purchase count are log-scaled inside the pipeline so one big-ticket order doesn't skew the read.

Customer Segmentation

A trained K-Means model, fit on scaled & PCA-reduced features, assigns each profile to one of two discovered segments.

Actionable Recommendations

Every segment ships with a plain-language business message so the result is ready to hand to a marketing team.
